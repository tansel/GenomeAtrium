The Atrium
Your genome as a room. The ring is the chromosomes, with variant density hanging below it.
Towers are findings, taller with more evidence. The gold band marks runs of homozygosity. Posts on the inner ring are your gene panels, coloured by coverage.
Arches overhead are structural variants. The cloud in the middle is the Landscape.
Drag to turn, wheel to zoom, hover to read. Click anything to open it in a window, with tabs for every 2D view.
